- TOTP (Time Based One Time Password) is basically getting a specific OTP for you based on the current "time"

- e.g: How earlier systems worked
	- You want to log in to your google account or your bank, you put in your email and password, the service provider receives that, then sends an OTP to you via cell phone or email.
	- This systems requires the service provide to first "generate" a new OTP, then send it to you somehow, then receive your submission to check if you've entered the correct OTP
	- This systems has the cost of sending receiving and generating the OTP
	- This system also requires reliable communication.
	- eg: If you have internet access, but your mobile phone is switched off/battery is dead => you can't receive OTP => you are locked out of your account

- In TOTP Systems:
	- When first setting up the TOTP, the service provider shares a "shared secret key" with you.
	- Whenever you want an OTP, the TOTP can be acquired on your local machine itself by performing some calculations on the shared secret key and current time values.
	- Now you send this OTP you calculated yourself
	- The service provider can also calculate and get the same OTP on their system
	- The only way this would've been possible is that you both had the same shared secret key and used the same time values and hence they can confirm it was you and log you in.


- The specification for the TOTP algorithm is given in [RFC 6238](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6238)


- According to the RFC
	- TOTP(K, C<sub>T</sub>) = HOTP(K, C<sub>T</sub>)
	- Where HOTP = HMAC Based OTP described in [RFC 4226](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc4226)
	- and HOTP(K, C) = Truncate(HMAC-SHA-1(K, C))

- HOTP basically used a counter (C) as it's second parameter to calculate the OTP
- TOTP is basically the same as HOTP but instead of a counter we use a time step value  (C<sub>T</sub>)

- So to implement TOTP from scratch and from the bottom-up:
	- We first implement SHA-1
	- Then somehow take the HMAC of that SHA-1???
	- Then "Truncate" the result of that
	- Then implement the "time" part C<sub>T</sub> part.
